# Owner Inputs and Approval Decisions

The repository already has presenter images, logos, an opening jingle, and
reference video clips. Do not request replacements unless a reviewed output
shows a specific gap.

## Needed before the first operating run

1. Confirm the current presenter personalities and draft voices in
   `assets/characters/CHARACTERS.yml`.
2. Identify the official X, Instagram, and YouTube handles and provide either
   read-only analytics exports/screenshots or approved OAuth access.
3. Define the first approval standard: acceptable factual risk, visual quality,
   voice quality, and brand tone.
4. Review one offline package: topic evidence, lead-video script, rendered
   video, carousel set, and reviewer verdict.

## Needed only when publishing is approved

- Connect platform accounts through official OAuth/UI.
- Configure publisher credentials through ignored environment files; never
  paste secrets into tracked documents.
- Approve posting cadence, time windows, and rollback/stop conditions.

## Not currently blocking

- More presenter images.
- New logos or a replacement jingle.
- Paid generation subscriptions.
- Automatic publishing.
