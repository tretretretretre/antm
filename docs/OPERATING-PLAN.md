# AINTM Operating Plan

## Current phase

Build a reliable, human-approved offline content package before automating
publication. The old RSS/Hacker News pipeline remains reusable scaffolding,
but it is not the topic-selection strategy.

## Daily chain

1. Researcher collects X, Instagram, and YouTube account/post observations,
   verifies claims, and stores a ranked evidence pack.
2. Strategist selects the best opportunity, presenter, format, hook, timing,
   hypothesis, and success metric.
3. Writer produces one lead-video script and multiple 9:16 carousel manifests.
4. Reviewer returns pass, revise, or reject with claim-level and brand checks.
5. Owner approves the topic and copy.
6. Production renders an offline package from existing assets and approved
   generation tools.
7. Owner approves final media separately before any publication.

## Build order

1. Canonical direction and safe model routing.
2. Social trend observation schema and persistent local database.
3. X, Instagram, and YouTube collectors with explicit unknown metrics.
4. Research-to-approval artifact contracts and validation.
5. Correct presenter rotation, brand assets, jingle, and 9:16 templates.
6. One daily offline lead video plus multiple carousels.
7. Performance ingestion, account-relative baselines, and learning loop.
8. Controlled publishing automation only after repeated owner approval.

## Approval boundaries

Unattended jobs may research and write ignored daily artifacts. They may not
modify production code, spend money, connect accounts, delete assets, publish,
or change credentials. Topic approval does not imply final-media approval.

## First milestone

Seven consecutive approval-ready daily packages, correct presenter rotation,
source-grounded claims, persistent observations, and enough comparable results
to identify at least one repeatable content pattern.
