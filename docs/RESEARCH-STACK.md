# AINTM Research Stack

## Primary surfaces

X, Instagram, and YouTube drive opportunity discovery and measurement.

- Grok/X Search: X-native accounts, posts, conversations, and early signals.
- YouTube search, Data API, and `yt-dlp`: channels, uploads, metadata, captions,
  transcripts, visible performance, and publishing cadence.
- Instagram: approved APIs, owner exports, browser review, or a narrowly scoped
  reviewed collector when permitted and necessary.

## Discovery and verification

- Tavily: broad discovery, extraction, and locating primary evidence.
- Official announcements, documentation, repositories, papers, release notes,
  and direct statements: final verification.
- NotebookLM: optional bulk comparison of selected sources; not a production
  dependency or primary evidence source.
- Apify: targeted reviewed Actor only when a specific platform gap remains.

RSS, Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, Google Trends, Product Hunt, and similar
sources are supporting signals. They must not outrank direct social evidence
merely because they are easier to collect.

## Stored observation

Record the platform, account, post, observation time, event time, topic,
content format, hook, structure, duration or slide count, CTA, posting time,
visible metrics, account baseline, relative performance, source URL, evidence
quality, and unknown fields. Never turn unavailable metrics into estimates.

## Candidate ranking

Rank on recency, relative attention velocity, independent confirmation,
follower-growth potential, novelty, audience payoff, visual potential, and
evidence quality. Apply a risk penalty for speculation, stale evidence, weak
attribution, or claims that cannot be expressed accurately in short form.

## Originality

Competitor content reveals demand, timing, hooks, information sequence, visual
structure, cadence, and CTA patterns. Verify the facts independently and build
a new AINTM thesis, script, sequence, design, and presentation. Do not lightly
paraphrase a creator transcript or recreate a post slide for slide.
